About Us

Craft Learned On Cathedrals, Passed On In The Classroom

We are a small outfit led by Patrick Rowland, working on traditional buildings across the West Country and beyond. Most of what we do is putting right work that was done decades or centuries ago, which means matching materials and methods rather than covering them over.

Every job is led by Patrick himself, from a weathered set of steps to the full restoration of a listed building. References are available on request.

Cement is harder than the stone around it, so moisture that would have escaped through the joint comes out through the face of the stone instead. That is what causes the crumbling and flaking you see on a lot of older walls. Lime mortar is softer than the stone and lets the wall dry out the way it was built to.
